## Assa-Abloy
#assa-abloy 

[../The entire Outer Angle section in this doc](Test%20Doorframes%20Angle%20Details.md#outer-angle)

After the inner angle is found, use its coordinates to mask out a very slim L-shaped portion of the cropped image, and use Canny edge detection with very low gradient thresholds (we are trying to find very soft edges) without using Otsu Thresholding on the image. The only processing done on the image is a Gaussian Blur to remove noise.

Works when there is an edge to detect. Sometimes the actual edge blends very neatly with the head behind it and is imperceptible, even to a human that is context-aware. The algorithm detects the machined edge in such cases.

Considering asking for two lamps shining diagonally so that reflection gives us a sharp edge detection. The horizontal edge is brighter than my future so the edge detection is working very well there. All these issues are only from the vertical edge.
